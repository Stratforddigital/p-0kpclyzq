          Questions, answered

          How is the price set? We arrange a free estimate visit to see the size, condition and cleaning needs of your space, then agree the scope and price with you. The prices above are where each clean starts.

          Does my request book the clean? It sends us the day you’d like for your estimate visit. We call or message to confirm it with you.

          Do you bring equipment? Yes. Equipment and supplies are included.

          Who are your cleaners? Uniformed, reference-checked cleaners, with training and insurance.

          What if something isn’t right? If an area falls short, tell us within 48 hours and we’ll return to re-clean it free of charge.

          Already have a quote? Bring it to us. Ask about our like-for-like price guarantee; we need a valid written quote.

          Do you clean businesses? Yes: offices and workplaces, shops and premises, communal areas, and one-off or deep cleans.

          Is there a minimum booking? Selected services have a minimum 30-minute booking.

          Where do you work? Groby, Anstey, Markfield, Newtown Linford and across the rest of Leicestershire.
